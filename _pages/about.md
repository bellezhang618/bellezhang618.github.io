**My research develops a relational view of strategic matching under uncertainty.** I show that relational position shapes which opportunities firms can interpret and act on, complementary configurations determine which matches succeed, and governance affects whether firms expect to capture enough value to search. These structures can create advantage, but they can also backfire when reliance on peers substitutes for independent judgment.
